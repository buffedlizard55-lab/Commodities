AUTOMATED FORWARD TEST · PAPER ONLY

A trading lab that shows its work.

Twenty-three rule-based strategies trade open Kalshi contracts on paper, twice an hour, from a scheduled collector that reads only the official public API. Every fill consumes a captured order book, every settlement comes from the official result, and every record is committed to this repository with its evidence. No made-up quotes, no silent fallbacks.

02 · placed & upcoming trades

Every placed simulation and every intended trade

Ledger folder on GitHub ↗Collector runs ↗
Explicit answerCan a strategy place an upcoming trade and simulate the real trading experience?

Yes, on paper and automatically: each cycle every rule scans the open contracts, writes its intent (contract, side, price, reason), fetches the fresh official order book, and fills as an immediate-or-cancel limit order against the displayed depth — VWAP, levels consumed, slippage versus the touch, exact fee and the unfilled remainder are recorded. Exits sell into the bid ladder; settlements use the official result and settlement_ts. No, it is not a live order: queue position, latency and market impact are not simulated as facts, so a real fill could differ.

11 · research method

Backtest only when the tape is real.

The project separates three states that are often mixed together: historical replay, live forward testing, and a research hypothesis. The UI never turns a hypothesis into a result.

01

Verify the event

Read the market ticker, status, close time, title, settlement terms and official API timestamp. A link to a third-party summary is not settlement evidence.

02

Verify the executable book

Fetch the open market’s order book, preserve the raw level prices and quantities, and consume depth level by level. A midpoint is a mark, not a fill.

03

Log the fill ledger

Store entry and exit times, side, VWAP, requested and filled size, level-by-level liquidity, slippage, fee assumption, source URL and market close time.

04

Explain the outcome

Leaderboard commentary is derived from observed fills. No fills means no return claim. The committed backtest uses only stored Kalshi candlesticks and official settlements, never reconstructed or invented bars; every future backtest must meet the same bar.
